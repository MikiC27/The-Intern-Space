    About The Intern Space

    The Intern Space is a free list of accounting and finance internships for students in Metro Detroit and across Michigan.

    Who runs it

    This site is independent. It is not run by or connected to any employer, university or student organization, and listing a firm here is not an endorsement.

    Where the listings come from

    Listings are collected from the firms' own career pages. Dates, requirements and availability can change at any time, and some postings do not list a close date. Always confirm the details on the posting itself before you apply.

    Questions and answers

    Questions are reviewed by a moderator before they appear. Answers are only posted when they can be backed by a cited source such as a school, government or professional site. If a question cannot be answered reliably, it is left unanswered.

    Recruiter contacts

    The Recruiters tab links to firms' official student recruiting pages. A named person is listed only with their consent and a link to where their contact details are published.

    Your privacy

    You sign in with Google or an email link so posts come from real people. Your email is used only for sign-in and, if you sign up, for deadline reminders, and it is visible only to the site's moderators. Account data is stored with Supabase, a database service. Questions and reports you send are visible only to moderators until they are approved.

    Contact, corrections and removal requests

    To report a wrong or outdated listing or contact, or to ask for something to be removed, use the Report button on a recruiter entry or send a message in the Ask a question tab. You need to be signed in, and a moderator will take a look.
